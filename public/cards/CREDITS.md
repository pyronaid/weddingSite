# Playing card faces

`<suit>/<rank>.svg` (rank 1 = Ace … 11 = Jack, 12 = Queen, 13 = King).

Artwork: Adrian Kennard's playing cards (https://www.me.uk/cards/), as packaged
by `@letele/playing-cards@0.1.0`. Released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain).

The SVGs were rendered once from that package's React components into static
files, so the site loads only the cards it shows and needs no extra dependency.

Change from the original: the Ace of spades (`spades/1.svg`) was simplified to
match the other aces (plain large pip), removing the artist's decorated spade,
QR code and website text.
